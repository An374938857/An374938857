常见问题

不注册账号能用吗？
能。不登录时全部功能可用，答题记录、错题本、学习进度都存在你自己的手机上。注册只是为了换设备时能接上之前的记录。

忘记密码了怎么办？
在登录页选「忘记密码」。如果这台设备或这个网络此前登录过该账号，可以直接重设；否则出于安全考虑需要走人工，请发邮件联系我们。

怎么换设备同步？
在新设备上用同一个账号登录即可。两个设备各自的记录会在登录后合并，不会互相覆盖。

怎么删除我的账号和全部数据？
应用内：我的 → 账号 → 删除账号，输入你的用户名确认。云端与本机的记录会一起永久删除，之后同一用户名无法再登录，我们也无法帮你找回。只想删掉某几条记录的话，在历史记录与错题本里逐条删除即可。

AI 陪练说的是专业建议吗？
不是。AI 陪练是开放式表达训练，帮你把思路讲清楚；它不做心理诊断，也不给治疗建议，回复由第三方大模型生成、仅供参考。请不要在对话里输入身份证号、手机号、银行卡号、住址或工作机密。

为什么必须联网才能用陪练？
陪练需要把你说的话交给大模型生成回复，所以要有网络。其他功能（体检、题库、学习中心、错题本）离线都能用。

报告里的分数是怎么算的？
按八个体检维度分别计分，并给出该维度上你的倾向与对应的思维框架。它是自我认识的参考，不是能力评定，也不构成任何专业结论。

Frequently asked

Do I need an account?
No. Everything works without signing in, and your history, wrong-answer notebook and progress stay on your device. An account only exists so your records follow you to another device.

I forgot my password.
Choose “Forgot password?” on the sign-in screen. If this device or network has signed in to the account before, you can reset it directly; otherwise, for safety, it needs a human — email us.

How do I move to a new device?
Sign in with the same account. The records from both devices are merged after signing in, not overwritten.

How do I delete my account and all my data?
In the app: Me → Account → Delete account, then type your username to confirm. The cloud and on-device records are permanently deleted together; afterwards the username can no longer sign in and we cannot restore it for you. If you only want to remove a few entries, delete them one by one in your history and wrong-answer notebook.

Is AI Coach giving me professional advice?
No. AI Coach is open-ended speaking practice that helps you explain your thinking. It does not diagnose and does not give treatment advice; replies are generated by a third-party model and are for reference only. Please don’t enter ID numbers, phone numbers, bank details, your address or confidential work information.

Why does coaching need a network connection?
Coaching sends what you write to a large language model to generate a reply, so it needs the network. Everything else — assessments, question banks, the learn hub, your wrong-answer notebook — works offline.

How is my report scored?
Each of the eight assessment dimensions is scored separately, and your result shows the tendency on that dimension together with the thinking frameworks that address it. It is a reference for self-understanding — not an ability rating, and not a professional conclusion of any kind.
