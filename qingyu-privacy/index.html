一句话

晴雨不收集你的任何数据。 你写下的、答过的、看过的，都只留在你自己的设备上。

我们收集什么

什么都不收集。 具体地说：

  • 不需要账号，没有注册、登录、手机号、邮箱。
  • 没有服务器。本产品没有后端，不存在「我们这边的数据库」。
  • 没有分析埋点，没有统计 SDK，没有广告 SDK，没有崩溃上报。
  • 不做设备标识，不读广告标识符，不做任何形式的用户画像。
  • 权限只有两项，都在你主动操作时才申请：「写入相册」——点「保存图片」存分享卡时；「通知」——你自己打开「每天轻轻问一句」时（默认关闭）。苹果健康的读写是三个独立开关、默认全部关闭，见下一节。

你的数据在哪里

  • iOS App：状态掠影、未写完的进度、天气贴纸册、心情记录与附言，全部存在这台设备上。
  • 网页版：存在你浏览器的 localStorage 里。

两者都只在本机，不上传、不备份、不同步。

如果你在 iOS 版里主动开启与苹果健康的同步，数据仍然只在这台设备上——只是从晴雨自己的沙盒，挪进了你自己那本可以授权给别的 App 看的册子。写入的只有「天气」，你写的那句话永远不会离开晴雨（苹果健康里没有存自由文本的地方）。

数据会离开设备吗

只在你自己主动分享时。 分享是唯一的数据出口，且完全由你决定：

  • 「保存图片」把分享卡写入你的相册；
  • 「分享图片」把图交给系统分享面板，由你选择发给谁；
  • 「复制文字」把文字放进系统剪贴板。

本产品不会自行发起任何网络请求。iOS 版不含任何网络代码。

与苹果健康的关系

三个开关，默认全部关闭，只有你自己打开才会工作：

  • 写进健康：把你在晴雨里记下的天气写成苹果健康里的「心理状态」。那句话本身不会被写进去，因为健康里没有这个字段。你在晴雨里删除某条记录时，晴雨写进健康的那条也会一并删除。
  • 读回健康：把你自己在苹果健康、Apple Watch 或 Journal 里记的心情读回来，和这里的记录放在一起看。读回来的那些是只读的——不是晴雨的数据，晴雨不能改也不能删。晴雨不缓存它们：你在健康里删掉的东西，晴雨里立刻就没了。
  • 身体读数：读你的心率变异性（HRV），相对你自己的基线给一句判断。它不是压力值，也不显示任何数字；大多数日子它什么都不显示，样本不够时也不显示。

这三项都不涉及网络。数据不经过任何服务器，苹果健康本身也在你的设备上。

你的控制

  • App 内「关于」页有两条独立的清空：「清空状态掠影与贴纸」与「清空心情记录」（后者会一并删除晴雨写进苹果健康里的那些）。
  • 卸载 App，或清除浏览器数据，即彻底删除，没有别处的副本——也正因为如此，删除后无法恢复。

求助热线

报告里会列出心理援助热线号码（中国大陆的三条）。点按号码会跳转系统电话，由你自己按下拨号；本产品不代拨、不记录。不在中国大陆的用户，报告里会给出 findahelpline.com，可查询所在国家或地区已验证的求助热线。

儿童

本产品面向成年人设计，不针对 13 周岁以下儿童，也不会收集儿童信息。

本政策的变更

若未来本产品引入任何需要联网或收集数据的功能，本政策会先更新，并在 App 内明确告知。在你看到并同意之前，不会收集任何数据。

免责

晴雨是帮助自我觉察的小工具，不是医疗诊断，也不能替代专业帮助。如果你持续难受，请一定和心理咨询师、医师，或信得过的人聊聊。

In one sentence

Sunshower collects nothing. What you write, answer and read stays only on your own device.

What we collect

Nothing at all. Specifically:

  • No account. No sign-up, no login, no phone number, no email address.
  • No server. There is no backend, so there is no database on our side.
  • No analytics, no tracking SDK, no advertising SDK, no crash reporting.
  • No device identifiers. We do not read the advertising identifier or build any kind of profile.
  • Two permissions, each requested only when you act. "Add to Photos" when you tap "Save image"; "Notifications" when you turn on the daily reminder yourself (off by default). Apple Health access is three separate switches, all off by default — see below.

Where your data lives

  • iOS app: snapshots, unfinished progress, your sticker book, mood entries and their notes all stay on this device.
  • Web version: stored in your browser's localStorage.

Both are local only — never uploaded, never backed up, never synced.

If you turn on Apple Health sync in the iOS app, your data is still only on this device — it has simply moved from Sunshower's own sandbox into your own book, which you can share with other apps. Only the weather is written; the line you write never leaves Sunshower (Health has nowhere to store free text).

Does data ever leave the device?

Only when you choose to share. Sharing is the single data path out, and it is entirely your decision:

  • "Save image" writes the share card to your Photos;
  • "Share image" hands the image to the system share sheet, and you choose the recipient;
  • "Copy text" places the text on the system clipboard.

The app never makes network requests on its own. The iOS version contains no networking code.

Apple Health

Three switches, all off by default — nothing happens until you turn them on:

  • Write to Health: saves the weather you log as a "State of Mind" entry in Health. The line you write is never included — Health has no field for it — and deleting an entry in Sunshower deletes the matching Health sample too.
  • Read from Health: brings back the moods you logged in Health, on Apple Watch or in Journal, so they sit alongside your own records. Those are read-only — they aren't Sunshower's data, so Sunshower can't edit or delete them. They are never cached: delete one in Health and it disappears here immediately.
  • Body readings: reads your heart rate variability, relative to your own baseline only. It is not a stress score and shows no numbers; most days it shows nothing at all, and it stays quiet when there isn't enough data.

None of this involves the network. No server is involved, and Health itself lives on your device.

Your controls

  • The About page has two separate delete actions: "Delete snapshots and stickers", and "Delete mood entries" (which also removes the samples Sunshower wrote to Health).
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing your browser data, deletes everything for good — there is no copy anywhere else. For the same reason, deleted data cannot be recovered.

Crisis helplines

The report lists psychological assistance helpline numbers (three in mainland China). Tapping a number opens the system phone app, and you press call yourself — the app never dials and never records. Users outside mainland China are given findahelpline.com, where verified helplines by country or region can be found.

Children

This app is designed for adults. It is not directed to children under 13 and does not collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds any feature that needs the network or collects data, this policy will be updated first and the change stated clearly in the app. Nothing will be collected before you have seen and agreed to it.

Disclaimer

Sunshower is a self-awareness tool. It is not a medical diagnosis and cannot replace professional help. If you keep struggling, please talk to a counsellor, a doctor, or someone you trust.
