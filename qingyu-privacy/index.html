一句话

晴雨不收集你的任何数据。 你写下的、答过的、看过的，都只留在你自己的设备上。

我们收集什么

什么都不收集。 具体地说：

  • 不需要账号，没有注册、登录、手机号、邮箱。
  • 没有服务器。本产品没有后端，不存在「我们这边的数据库」。
  • 没有分析埋点，没有统计 SDK，没有广告 SDK，没有崩溃上报。
  • 不做设备标识，不读广告标识符，不做任何形式的用户画像。
  • 不需要任何敏感权限。唯一申请的权限是「写入相册」——只在你主动点「保存图片」把分享卡存到相册时用，图片不会离开你的设备。

你的数据在哪里

  • iOS App：状态掠影、未写完的进度、天气贴纸册，全部存在 App 自己的沙盒里。
  • 网页版：存在你浏览器的 localStorage 里。

两者都只在本机，不上传、不备份、不同步。

数据会离开设备吗

只在你自己主动分享时。 分享是唯一的数据出口，且完全由你决定：

  • 「保存图片」把分享卡写入你的相册；
  • 「分享图片」把图交给系统分享面板，由你选择发给谁；
  • 「复制文字」把文字放进系统剪贴板。

本产品不会自行发起任何网络请求。iOS 版不含任何网络代码。

你的控制

  • App 内「关于」页有「清空全部记录」，一键删除全部数据。
  • 卸载 App，或清除浏览器数据，即彻底删除，没有别处的副本——也正因为如此，删除后无法恢复。

求助热线

报告里会列出心理援助热线号码（中国大陆的三条）。点按号码会跳转系统电话，由你自己按下拨号；本产品不代拨、不记录。不在中国大陆的用户，报告里会给出 findahelpline.com，可查询所在国家或地区已验证的求助热线。

儿童

本产品面向成年人设计，不针对 13 周岁以下儿童，也不会收集儿童信息。

本政策的变更

若未来本产品引入任何需要联网或收集数据的功能，本政策会先更新，并在 App 内明确告知。在你看到并同意之前，不会收集任何数据。

免责

晴雨是帮助自我觉察的小工具，不是医疗诊断，也不能替代专业帮助。如果你持续难受，请一定和心理咨询师、医师，或信得过的人聊聊。

In one sentence

Sunshower collects nothing. What you write, answer and read stays only on your own device.

What we collect

Nothing at all. Specifically:

  • No account. No sign-up, no login, no phone number, no email address.
  • No server. There is no backend, so there is no database on our side.
  • No analytics, no tracking SDK, no advertising SDK, no crash reporting.
  • No device identifiers. We do not read the advertising identifier or build any kind of profile.
  • No sensitive permissions. The only permission we request is "add to Photos" — used solely when you tap "Save image" to store the share card, and the image never leaves your device.

Where your data lives

  • iOS app: snapshots, unfinished progress and your sticker book are stored in the app's own sandbox.
  • Web version: stored in your browser's localStorage.

Both are local only — never uploaded, never backed up, never synced.

Does data ever leave the device?

Only when you choose to share. Sharing is the single data path out, and it is entirely your decision:

  • "Save image" writes the share card to your Photos;
  • "Share image" hands the image to the system share sheet, and you choose the recipient;
  • "Copy text" places the text on the system clipboard.

The app never makes network requests on its own. The iOS version contains no networking code.

Your controls

  • The About page has "Delete all records", which erases everything in one tap.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing your browser data, deletes everything for good — there is no copy anywhere else. For the same reason, deleted data cannot be recovered.

Crisis helplines

The report lists psychological assistance helpline numbers (three in mainland China). Tapping a number opens the system phone app, and you press call yourself — the app never dials and never records. Users outside mainland China are given findahelpline.com, where verified helplines by country or region can be found.

Children

This app is designed for adults. It is not directed to children under 13 and does not collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds any feature that needs the network or collects data, this policy will be updated first and the change stated clearly in the app. Nothing will be collected before you have seen and agreed to it.

Disclaimer

Sunshower is a self-awareness tool. It is not a medical diagnosis and cannot replace professional help. If you keep struggling, please talk to a counsellor, a doctor, or someone you trust.
