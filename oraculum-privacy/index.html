一句话

没有账号，没有追踪。 起卦完全在你的设备上完成，不需要联网。只有当你主动点「AI 解读」时，你填写的姓名、生日与问题才会离开设备，用于生成那一次解读。

我们收集什么

发起一次占卜时，你自己填写的以下信息会用于本次起卦与解签：

  • 姓名（通常是昵称，用于把解读写得像在对你说话）；
  • 生日（可以不填）；
  • 你输入的问题；
  • 你选的类别（如事业、情感、健康）。

除此之外，本应用不要求也不收集账号资料、手机号、邮箱、通讯录、位置，也没有支付。

数据什么时候离开你的设备

只有一种情况：你主动请求 AI 解读时。

  • 起卦不上网。 摇卦、排卦、卦辞与六爻都在你的设备上用内置的六十四卦文本完成，不需要网络。
  • AI 解读会上网。 你点「AI 解读」后，上面列出的姓名、生日、问题、所选类别，连同起得的卦，会被发送给为我们提供大模型能力的第三方服务商 DeepSeek，由它生成那段白话解读。
  • 经由我们的中转服务。 请求链路是：你的设备 → 我们自有的中转服务 → DeepSeek。中转服务只做转发与用量计数，不保存你问题的原文，也不保存姓名与生日。它存在的意义是不让厂商密钥进入 App。
  • 解读结果缓存在本地。 生成好的解读会存进你这台设备的历史记录里，方便你回看，不会重复发送。
  • 不用作别的用途。 我们不用这些信息做广告、用户画像、行为分析，也不出售、不共享给除上述大模型服务之外的任何一方。

关于 DeepSeek 自身如何处理这些数据，以其隐私政策与数据处理条款为准，可在 platform.deepseek.com 查阅。

In one sentence

No account, no tracking. The hexagram is cast entirely on your device and needs no network. Only when you tap "AI reading" does what you typed — name, birth date, question — leave the device, and only to produce that one reading.

What we collect

When you start a divination, the following is used for that casting and reading, and nothing else:

  • Name (usually a nickname, so the reading can speak to you directly);
  • Birth date (optional);
  • The question you write;
  • The categories you pick (career, relationships, health, and so on).

Beyond that, the app asks for and collects no account details, phone number, email, contacts or location. There is no payment.

When data leaves your device

In exactly one situation: when you ask for an AI reading.

  • Casting does not go online. Shaking, building the hexagram, and the judgments, images and line texts all come from the sixty-four hexagrams bundled with the app. No network is involved.
  • The AI reading does. When you tap "AI reading", the name, birth date, question and categories listed above, together with the hexagram you cast, are sent to DeepSeek, the third-party provider that supplies the language model, so it can write that reading.
  • It goes through our relay. The path is: your device → our own relay service → DeepSeek. The relay only forwards the request and counts usage. It does not store the text of your question, your name or your birth date. Its purpose is to keep vendor keys out of the app.
  • The reading is cached on your device. Once generated, the reading is saved into your local history so you can revisit it without sending the request again.
  • No other use. We do not use this information for advertising, profiling or behavioural analysis, and we do not sell or share it with anyone other than the language-model provider named above.

How DeepSeek itself handles the data is governed by its own privacy policy and data processing terms, available at platform.deepseek.com.

What works without a network

  • Casting and displaying the hexagram;
  • The judgments, images, line texts and category readings for all sixty-four hexagrams (bundled with the app);
  • History, the collection card, and share images.

All of these work offline. Only the AI reading needs the network; without it, the result screen falls back to the built-in curator's reading.

Where your data lives

  • iOS app: your language preference and divination history (including generated readings) are stored in the app's own sandbox and are removed when you delete the app.
  • Web version: stored in your browser's localStorage.

Both are on your device only — never uploaded, never synced, and unreadable by us.

Permissions

One only: adding to your photo library, and only when you tap "Save image" on a collection card. The app does not ask for camera, microphone, location, contacts or notifications.

Third-party services

  • DeepSeek (api.deepseek.com): receives the information described above, and only when you request an AI reading.
  • Google Fonts: loaded by the web version only, from fonts.googleapis.com. The iOS app ships its fonts inside the bundle and fetches nothing.

What we do not do

  • No accounts — no sign-up, sign-in or password recovery;
  • No ads and no advertising SDKs;
  • No third-party analytics SDKs and no crash-reporting SDKs;
  • No advertising identifier (IDFA), no device fingerprinting, no user profiling;
  • No push notifications.

Your controls

  • Delete local data: remove history inside the app, or simply delete the app; on the web, clear site data. Once removed, local records cannot be recovered.
  • Ask us to delete, or ask us anything: we do not retain the text of your question ourselves. If you have a question, or want help with something you submitted, write to the address at the foot of this page.

This app is intended for adults. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe we have received information from a child, contact us and we will delete it.
