AI 解读出不来怎么办

按顺序排查：

  • 检查网络：AI 解读需要联网，其余功能不需要。断网时结果页会显示内置的策展人解读，这是正常的降级，不是故障。
  • 稍后再试：解读由第三方大模型服务生成，偶发拥堵或限流时会失败。
  • 看看是不是重复的问题：同一个问题重复查看会直接读本地缓存，不消耗额度，也不会重新生成。

若持续失败，请写信告诉我们，并附上你使用的 iPhone/iPad 型号与系统版本。

起卦的结果是随机的吗

是。摇卦用的是设备上的随机数，每次结果不同。同一卦的解读文本是固定的（来自内置语料），AI 那段则会结合你问的问题重新生成。

我的数据在哪里

语言偏好与占卜历史只存在这台设备上；起卦不需要联网。只有你主动请求 AI 解读时，姓名、生日与问题才会离开设备。完整说明见隐私政策。

怎么删除我的记录

在 App 内的历史记录里删除单条，或直接卸载 App——本地记录会一并消失，且无法恢复。我们这边不留存你问题的原文，所以也不需要另外申请注销。

藏品卡存不进相册

首次点「存储图像」时系统会询问相册权限，需要允许。若之前拒绝过，去「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 照片」里把这个 App 的权限改为「添加照片」即可。

还想要别的语言

界面提供简体中文、繁體中文与 English，可在设置里随时切换。

What this is

ORACVLVM · The Sixty-Four Hexagrams is an exhibition you take part in by hand. You write down a question you actually care about, cast a hexagram, read its judgment and its six lines, receive a reading in plain language, and keep a collection card of your own.

It is a cultural and entertainment piece, not a fortune-telling service, and it offers no medical, legal, financial or psychological advice. See the disclaimer in the privacy policy.

The AI reading didn't appear

Work through these in order:

  • Check your connection. The AI reading needs the network; nothing else does. Offline, the result screen shows the built-in curator's reading — that is a deliberate fallback, not a fault.
  • Try again shortly. Readings are generated by a third-party language-model service, which occasionally throttles or gets busy.
  • Is it a repeat? Revisiting the same question reads from the local cache — no quota is spent and nothing is regenerated.

If it keeps failing, write to us with your iPhone or iPad model and system version.

Is the casting random?

Yes. The shake uses the device's random number generator, so every casting differs. The classical readings for each hexagram are fixed text bundled with the app; only the AI passage is written afresh around your question.

Where is my data?

Your language preference and divination history stay on this device, and casting needs no network. Your name, birth date and question leave the device only when you ask for an AI reading. Full detail is in the privacy policy.

How do I delete my records?

Delete individual entries in the app's history, or simply delete the app — local records go with it and cannot be recovered. We do not retain the text of your questions, so there is no separate account to close.

The collection card won't save to Photos

The first time you tap "Save image" the system asks for photo permission, which you need to allow. If you declined earlier, go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos and set this app to "Add Photos Only".

Other languages

The interface is available in Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese and English, switchable at any time in settings.
