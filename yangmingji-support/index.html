数据与同步

需要注册或登录吗？ 不需要。阳明集没有账号系统，也没有服务器。

我的阅读进度、收藏存在哪里？ 只存在这台设备上、应用自己的沙盒内。桌面小组件要显示「最近收藏」与「续读」，会另存一份状态快照在本机的 App Group 容器里，同样不离开设备。

换手机或重装后，进度能恢复吗？ 不能。因为没有账号与云端，数据不在任何别的地方——卸载或换机即彻底消失，且无法找回。这是「不收集数据」的另一面。

能同步到 iCloud / 多设备共用吗？ 目前不支持。

小组件

组件是空白，或者写着「尚无收藏」？ 「收藏」与「续读」两类组件读的是应用写下的状态快照：先在应用里藏下至少一条、或标记至少一条已读，再回桌面看。若仍为空，打开一次应用即可触发快照写入。

刚藏下的一条，桌面上没变？ 桌面刷新由系统调度，不总是即时。打开一次应用会主动请求重载；也可以稍等片刻，或把组件移除后重新添加。

小组件上的字被截断了吗？ 不应该。中 / 大档会自动缩到「整条排得下」的字号，实在排不下的长文才按句读收口并加省略号；小档只作入口、不排正文，也不允许出现半个字。若你看到被切断的字，请截图发给我们。

组件上的「心解」胶囊点不动？ 请确认是在桌面上直接点组件本体——翻面由系统重排完成，需要组件已经加载过。若长时间无响应，移除后重新添加即可。

阅读

需要联网吗？会不会偷偷上传什么？ 不需要，也不会。全部内容与字体都随安装包分发，飞行模式下与联网时完全一样；应用内不含任何网络代码，也就没有可上传的通道。

字太小，怎么放大？ 应用内的正文会跟随系统的「文字大小」设置。到「设置 → 显示与亮度 → 文字大小」调大即可（应用内「关于」页亦有说明）。

昼读 / 夜读怎么切？ 在「关于」页的「外观」一栏选「跟随系统 / 昼读 / 夜读」。

书房里的书卷点不开、条目不见了？ 书房的卷与节可以折叠，可能只是被收起来了，点一下卷签或节标题即可展开。若确实有异常，请截图告诉我们。

日签

怎么生成日签？ 在条目详情页底部，已读或已收藏的条目可以生成日签卡片。

日签会存到哪？ 由你决定：「保存图片」写入相册，「分享图片」交给系统分享面板，「复制文字」放进剪贴板。应用本身不读取相册，也不代你分享。

内容

正文是什么来源？ 《传习录》以中文维基文库通行本为底，条目编号沿用既有编号体系；《大学》《中庸》取自开源语料库 chinese-poetry。原文为公版，繁体源文统一转为简体呈现，异文存其原貌。

白话、注释、心解是谁写的？ 全部为本读本原创，未采用任何现有今译本。

我发现了一个错字或疑问，怎么反馈？ 欢迎写信到 an1023@outlook.com，注明条目编号（如「传习录 卷上 第 5 条」）会方便定位。校勘问题会逐条核对。

Data and sync

Do I need an account? No. Yangmingji has no account system and no server.

Where are my reading progress and favourites stored? Only on this device, inside the app's own sandbox. The Home Screen widget needs to show "most recent favourite" and "continue reading", so a state snapshot is also written to a local App Group container on the same device. It never leaves the device either.

If I switch phones or reinstall, is my progress restored? No. With no account and no cloud, nothing exists anywhere else — deleting the app or changing devices erases it for good, and it cannot be recovered. That is the other side of collecting nothing.

Can it sync to iCloud or across devices? Not at present.

Widgets

The widget is blank, or says "no favourites yet"? The Favourites and Continue Reading widgets read the state snapshot the app writes. Save at least one passage, or mark at least one as read, then go back to the Home Screen. If it is still empty, open the app once to trigger a fresh snapshot.

I just saved a passage but the widget has not changed? Home Screen refreshes are scheduled by the system and are not always immediate. Opening the app asks for a reload; otherwise wait a moment, or remove and re-add the widget.

Is the widget text truncated? It should not be. The medium and large sizes step the type size down until the whole passage fits; only genuinely long passages are closed off at a punctuation mark with an ellipsis. The small size is an entry point only and never shows body text, so no half characters should appear there. If you do see cut-off text, please send us a screenshot.

The "commentary" capsule on the widget does not respond? Make sure you are tapping the widget body itself on the Home Screen — flipping is performed by a system timeline reload, so the widget must have loaded at least once. If it stays unresponsive, remove and re-add it.

Reading

Does it need the network? Does it upload anything? No, and no. All content and both fonts ship inside the app package, so it behaves identically in Airplane Mode; there is no networking code in the app, and therefore no channel through which anything could be uploaded.

The text is too small — can I enlarge it? Body text follows the system text size. Go to Settings → Display & Brightness → Text Size (also noted in the app under About).

How do I switch between day and night reading? In the app, open About and choose Follow System / Day / Night under Appearance.

Volumes or passages in the library will not open? Volumes and sections in the library collapse; they may simply be folded away. Tap the volume tab or section heading to expand. If something is genuinely wrong, please send a screenshot.

Daily card

How do I make a daily card? At the bottom of a passage, any passage you have read or saved can be turned into a daily card.

Where does the card go? Your choice: "Save image" writes it to Photos, "Share image" hands it to the system share sheet, "Copy text" puts it on the clipboard. The app itself never reads your Photo library and never shares on your behalf.

Content

Where do the texts come from? Chuanxilu is based on the standard edition in Chinese Wikisource, keeping its existing passage numbering; Daxue and Zhongyong come from the open-source chinese-poetry corpus. The source texts are public domain; traditional-character sources are rendered in simplified characters, and variant readings are preserved as they stand.

Who wrote the translations, notes and commentaries? All of them are original to this edition. No existing modern translation has been used.

I found a typo or have a question about the text — how do I report it? Email an1023@outlook.com; quoting the passage number (e.g. "Chuanxilu, Volume I, No. 5") helps a lot. Textual questions are checked one by one.
