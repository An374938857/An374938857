数据与隐私

需要注册或登录吗？ 不需要。晴雨没有账号系统，也没有服务器。

我的记录存在哪里？ 只存在这台设备上、应用自己的沙盒里。不上传、不备份、不同步。iOS 版不含任何网络代码，飞行模式下与联网时完全一样。

换手机或重装后能恢复吗？ 状态掠影、未写完的进度、贴纸册不能恢复——它们没有别处的副本。心情记录如果开着「写进健康」，记下的天气会跟着苹果健康回来；你写的那句话不会。

怎么把数据全删掉？ 「关于」页有两条独立的清空：「清空状态掠影与贴纸」与「清空心情记录」（后者会一并删掉晴雨写进苹果健康里的那些）。删除后无法恢复。

苹果健康

怎么打开？ 「关于」页的「苹果健康」一栏。三个开关默认全关，只有你自己打开才会工作。

写进去的是什么？ 只有天气，写成苹果健康里的「心理状态」。你写的那句话不会被写进去——健康里没有存自由文本的字段。你在晴雨里删掉某条记录，写进健康的那条也会一并删除。

「读回你在健康里记的心情」读到的是什么？ 你自己在苹果健康、Apple Watch 或 Journal 里记的心情。那些是只读的，晴雨不能改也不能删，也不缓存——你在健康里删掉，晴雨里立刻就没了。

「身体读数」为什么大多数时候什么都不显示？ 它读的是心率变异性（HRV），相对你自己的基线给一句判断。样本不够时（比如没有 Apple Watch）它就不显示。它不是压力值，也不显示任何数字。

提示「没有拿到写入健康的权限」？ 到系统「设置 → 健康 → 数据访问」里允许晴雨写入。

提醒

怎么打开？ 「关于」页「提醒」一栏打开「每天轻轻问一句」，再选时间。默认是关的。

已经记过了还会提醒吗？ 不会。已经记过的那一天不会再提醒——它不会变成打卡。在桌面上记完一条，当天那条提醒也会一并取消。

没收到通知？ 先看系统「设置 → 通知 → 晴雨」是否允许，再确认应用内的开关是开的。

小组件

组件上是空的，或者点不动？ 打开一次晴雨即可。桌面刷新由系统调度，不总是即时；也可以把组件移除后重新添加。

在组件上记了一条，打开应用却没看到？ 切回前台时晴雨会重读一次共享的记录。若仍不一致，打开一次应用就能同步。

报告与求助

报告是什么？ 一份自我觉察的掠影，不是诊断，也不打分。它只描述你最近的状态，不给结论。

为什么报告里会有热线号码？ 如果某些回答命中安全信号，报告顶部会出现一张求助卡，列出心理援助热线。号码点按后跳转系统电话，由你自己按下拨号——晴雨不代拨、不记录。

不在中国大陆？ 报告里会给出 findahelpline.com，可查询所在国家或地区已验证的求助热线。

语言与外观

怎么换语言？ 「关于」页「语言」一栏：跟随系统 / 简体中文 / 繁體中文 / English。

怎么换深浅色？ 「关于」页「外观」一栏。浅色下天光最完整——四档天气、太阳沉下去、云压下来都在那里看得最清楚；深色是夜里那版。

Data and privacy

Do I need to sign up? No. Sunshower has no account system and no server.

Where are my records stored? Only on this device, in the app's own sandbox. Never uploaded, never backed up, never synced. The iOS version contains no networking code, so it behaves identically in Airplane Mode and online.

Can I recover my data after switching phones or reinstalling? Snapshots, unfinished progress and your sticker book cannot be recovered — there is no copy anywhere else. Mood entries are different: with "Write to Health" on, the weather you logged comes back with Apple Health. The line you wrote does not.

How do I delete everything? The About page has two separate delete actions: "Delete snapshots and stickers", and "Delete mood entries" (which also removes the samples Sunshower wrote to Health). Deletion cannot be undone.

Apple Health

How do I turn it on? In the "Apple Health" section of the About page. All three switches are off by default — nothing happens until you turn them on.

What gets written? Only the weather, saved as a "State of Mind" entry. The line you write is never written — Health has no field for free text. Delete an entry in Sunshower and the corresponding Health sample is deleted too.

What does "Read back the moods you logged in Health" read? The moods you logged yourself in Apple Health, on Apple Watch or in Journal. Those are read-only — Sunshower can't edit or delete them, and never caches them: delete one in Health and it disappears here immediately.

Why does "Body readings" mostly show nothing? It reads your heart rate variability (HRV) and gives one line relative to your own baseline. When there isn't enough data — for example without an Apple Watch — it stays silent. It is not a stress score and shows no numbers.

It says "no permission to write to Health". Go to Settings → Health → Data Access and allow Sunshower to write.

Daily reminder

How do I turn it on? In the "Reminder" section of the About page, turn on "A gentle question each day" and pick a time. It is off by default.

Will it remind me if I already logged? No. A day you have already logged is never reminded again — it never turns into a streak. Logging from the Home Screen widget also cancels that day's reminder.

I'm not getting notifications. First check Settings → Notifications → Sunshower is allowed, then confirm the in-app switch is on.

Widget

The widget is blank, or taps do nothing? Open Sunshower once. Home Screen refreshes are scheduled by the system and aren't always immediate; you can also remove the widget and add it again.

I logged from the widget but the app doesn't show it? Sunshower re-reads the shared store when it returns to the foreground. If it still looks off, opening the app once will sync it.

Reports and getting help

What is the report? A snapshot for self-reflection. It is not a diagnosis and it does not score you. It describes your recent state without drawing conclusions.

Why does a helpline appear in the report? If some answers trigger a safety signal, a support card appears at the top of the report listing psychological assistance helplines. Tapping a number opens the system phone app and you press call yourself — Sunshower never dials and never records.

I'm not in mainland China. The report gives findahelpline.com, where you can find verified helplines for your country or region.

Language and appearance

How do I change the language? The "Language" section of the About page: Follow system / 简体中文 / 繁體中文 / English.

How do I change light and dark? The "Appearance" section of the About page. The light version is where the sky reads most completely — all four weather states, the sun going down, the clouds pressing in; dark is the night version.
