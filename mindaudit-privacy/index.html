Mind Audit Privacy Policy · 生效日期 / Effective 2026-09-16 · an1023@outlook.com

简体 English

一句话

思维测验是自助练习工具，不做诊断、不提供治疗。 不登录就能用，所有数据留在你自己的手机上；只有你主动注册账号并开启云同步，记录才会存到我们的服务器。

我们收集什么

1. 不登录时：什么都不上传。 答题记录、错题本、学习进度都存在设备本地。应用会发送一条不含内容的匿名统计（见下）。

2. 注册账号后：

  • 用户名与密码（密码经 PBKDF2 加盐派生后存储，我们不保存明文，也无法还原）；
  • 用 Apple 登录时，我们只保存 Apple 提供的匿名用户标识用于识别你的账号。你可以选择不把邮箱共享给我们，此时我们拿不到你的邮箱；
  • 你主动同步的使用记录：体检记录、答题历史、学习进度、错题本、AI 陪练会话。

匿名使用统计

为了知道哪些功能真的有人用，应用会发送匿名事件（例如「完成一次体检」「打开学习中心」）。这条记录包含：事件名称、一个随机生成的设备标识（不是广告标识符，不含任何硬件信息）、IP 地址与时间。

它不包含你的答题内容、对话内容，也不包含你的账号名。 它只用于统计功能使用量与排查故障，不用于广告，不做用户画像，不追踪你在其他应用或网站上的行为。本应用没有接入任何第三方广告或分析 SDK。

AI 陪练对话（重要）

「AI 陪练」会把你在对话中输入的文本，发送给第三方大模型服务商处理，以生成教练的回复。截至本政策更新日，模型为 DeepSeek，经 API 服务商 api.commandcode.ai 接入。

  • 对话文本会离开我们的服务器，传输给该第三方服务商，其处理同时受该服务商自身隐私政策的约束；
  • 请不要在对话中输入身份证号、手机号、银行卡号、家庭住址、工作机密等敏感信息；建议聚焦于工作方法、思维框架这类话题；
  • AI 生成的内容仅供参考，不构成任何专业建议，也不具备诊断或治疗作用。

如果你正经历强烈的痛苦，或有伤害自己的念头：请找一个真实的人聊聊。可通过 findahelpline.com 查找你所在地区的免费求助热线；若处于紧急危险中，请立刻拨打你所在地区的急救电话。应用内「AI 陪练」页面右上角也常驻了同一个求助入口。

我们不收集什么

  • 不读通讯录、相册、定位、麦克风、相机，相关系统权限一概不申请；
  • 不读广告标识符（IDFA），不做跨应用追踪；
  • 不向任何第三方出售或出租你的个人数据。 除上述 AI 陪练所需的文本外，我们不会把你的数据提供给第三方，法律法规要求披露的情形除外。

数据存在哪里、存多久

  • 账号与同步数据存放在我们自有的云服务器上，传输使用 HTTPS；
  • 你退出登录后，本机记录仍保留在设备上（下次登录可继续同步）；
  • 你删除账号后，云端的账号记录、同步数据与陪练会话会被立即删除，本机记录同时清除，且不可恢复。

你的控制

  • 删除账号（自助，无需联系我们）：应用内「我的 → 账号 → 删除账号」，输入用户名确认即可。删除是真删，不是标记——云端记录被抹除，之后用同一用户名无法再登录，我们也无法帮你找回。
  • 想删单条记录：答题历史、错题本都可以在应用内逐条删除。
  • 不想让对话离开设备：不要使用 AI 陪练即可，其余功能不受影响。
  • 你也可以发邮件到 an1023@outlook.com 行使上述任何一项权利。

儿童

本产品面向成年人，不针对 13 周岁以下儿童设计，也不会主动收集儿童的个人信息。若发现误收集，请联系我们删除。

本政策的变更

本政策若有更新，会修改页首的生效日期；涉及数据用途的重大变更，会在应用内显著提示。

In one sentence

Mind Audit is a self-help practice tool. It does not diagnose and it does not treat. You can use it without an account, and everything stays on your device. Records only reach our servers if you create an account and turn on cloud sync.

What we collect

1. Signed out — nothing is uploaded. Your quiz history, wrong-answer notebook and learning progress stay on your device. The app does send one anonymous, content-free usage event (see below).

2. After you register:

  • Your username and password (passwords are stored as a salted PBKDF2 derivation — we never keep the plaintext and cannot recover it);
  • If you sign in with Apple, we store only the anonymous user identifier Apple gives us, so we can recognise your account. You may choose not to share your email, in which case we never receive it;
  • The usage records you sync: assessments, quiz history, learning progress, wrong-answer notebook and AI coaching sessions.

Anonymous usage stats

So we know which features are actually used, the app sends anonymous events (for example “completed an assessment”). Each contains an event name, a randomly generated device identifier (not an advertising identifier, and no hardware information), an IP address and a timestamp.

It contains none of your answers, none of your conversations, and not your username. It is used only to measure feature usage and to debug failures — never for advertising, never for profiling, and never to track you across other apps or websites. This app does not include any third-party advertising or analytics SDK.

AI coaching conversations (important)

“AI Coach” sends the text you type in a session to a third-party large-language-model provider in order to generate the coach’s reply. As of the date of this policy the model is DeepSeek, reached through the API provider api.commandcode.ai.

  • The text leaves our servers and is transmitted to that third party, whose own privacy policy also applies to its processing;
  • Please do not enter ID numbers, phone numbers, bank details, your home address or confidential work information. Keep it to work methods and thinking frameworks;
  • AI output is for reference only. It is not professional advice and has no diagnostic or therapeutic function.

If you are in serious distress, or having thoughts of harming yourself: please talk to a real person. You can find free crisis lines by country at findahelpline.com; if you are in immediate danger, call your local emergency number right away. The same link is always available from the top of the AI Coach screen.

What we do not collect

  • We do not read your contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera, and we request none of those permissions;
  • We do not read the advertising identifier (IDFA) and do no cross-app tracking;
  • We never sell or rent your personal data. Apart from the text required for AI coaching, we share nothing with third parties except where the law requires it.

Where data lives, and for how long

  • Account and synced data are stored on our own cloud servers, transmitted over HTTPS;
  • Signing out leaves your on-device records in place, ready to sync again next time;
  • Deleting your account removes the account record, synced data and coaching sessions from the cloud immediately, and clears the on-device records at the same time. It cannot be undone.

Your controls

  • Delete your account yourself, without contacting us: in the app, go to “Me → Account → Delete account” and type your username to confirm. This is a real deletion, not a flag — the cloud records are erased, the username can no longer sign in, and we cannot restore it for you.
  • Delete individual records: quiz history and wrong-answer entries can be removed one by one in the app.
  • Keep conversations on-device: simply don’t use AI Coach; everything else still works.
  • You can also email an1023@outlook.com to exercise any of these rights.

Children

This product is intended for adults. It is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes we update the effective date above. Material changes to how data is used are announced prominently inside the app.
