一句话

阳明集不收集你的任何数据。 读到哪一条、藏下哪一条、用哪种外观，都只留在你自己的设备上。

我们收集什么

什么都不收集。 具体地说：

  • 不需要账号，没有注册、登录、手机号、邮箱。
  • 没有服务器。本应用没有后端，也不存在「我们这边的数据库」。
  • 不发起任何网络请求。应用内含有的全部内容（正文、白话、注释、心解、字体）都随安装包分发，装好即可读，飞行模式下与联网时完全一样。
  • 没有分析埋点，没有统计 SDK，没有广告 SDK，没有崩溃上报。
  • 不做设备标识，不读广告标识符，不做任何形式的用户画像。
  • 不需要任何系统权限。不请求相册、通讯录、定位、麦克风、相机、通知。

你的数据在哪里

只有三样东西会被记下来，且都存在应用自己的沙盒内：

  • 阅读进度：哪几条已读；
  • 收藏：你藏下了哪几条；
  • 呈现偏好：昼读 / 夜读 / 跟随系统，以及书房的折叠状态。

此外，桌面小组件要显示「最近收藏」与「续读」，需要读到上面这两样状态；应用因此把一个状态快照写进本机的一个 App Group 容器，供小组件读取。这个容器只在这台设备上，只有本应用与它自己的小组件能访问，不上传、不备份到我们的服务器（我们也没有服务器）、不以任何方式离开设备。

数据会离开设备吗

只有你主动分享时。日签导出与分享由你决定：

  • 「保存图片」把日签卡片写入你的相册（这一步由系统相册完成，应用不读取相册）；
  • 「分享图片」把图交给系统分享面板，由你选择发给谁；
  • 「复制文字」把文字放进系统剪贴板。

除这几条由你亲手触发的路径外，本应用不会把任何数据送出设备。

你的控制

  • 收藏与已读都可以在应用内逐条取消。
  • 卸载应用即彻底删除全部记录，没有别处的副本——也正因为如此，删除后无法恢复。

字体与文本

  • 随包的两款字体按 SIL Open Font License 1.1 使用，应用内「关于 → 开源许可」附有许可全文与版权行。
  • 正文本体为公版文本（王阳明卒于 1529 年），来源与校勘口径见应用内「关于」。

儿童

本应用面向一般读者，不针对 13 周岁以下儿童，也不会收集儿童信息。

本政策的变更

若未来本应用引入任何需要联网或收集数据的功能，本政策会先更新，并在应用内明确告知。在你看到并同意之前，不会收集任何数据。

In one sentence

Yangmingji collects nothing. What you read, what you save and how you like it displayed stays only on your own device.

What we collect

Nothing at all. Specifically:

  • No account. No sign-up, no login, no phone number, no email address.
  • No server. There is no backend, so there is no database on our side.
  • No network requests of any kind. Everything the app contains — the source texts, translations, notes, commentaries and the bundled fonts — ships inside the app package. It reads the same in Airplane Mode as it does online.
  • No analytics, no tracking SDK, no advertising SDK, no crash reporting.
  • No device identifiers. We do not read the advertising identifier or build any kind of profile.
  • No system permissions. We do not ask for Photos, Contacts, Location, Microphone, Camera or Notifications.

Where your data lives

Only three things are ever recorded, and all of them live inside the app's own sandbox:

  • Reading progress: which passages you have read;
  • Favourites: which passages you have saved;
  • Display preferences: day / night / follow-system, and how your library shelves are collapsed.

In addition, the Home Screen widget needs to show "most recent favourite" and "continue reading", which means reading the two states above. The app therefore writes a state snapshot into a local App Group container for the widget to read. That container exists only on this device, is reachable only by this app and its own widget, is never uploaded, is never backed up to a server of ours (we have no server), and never leaves the device in any form.

Does data ever leave the device?

Only when you choose to share. Exporting and sharing a daily card is entirely your decision:

  • "Save image" writes the daily card to your Photos (the system photo library does this; the app does not read your Photo library);
  • "Share image" hands the image to the system share sheet, and you choose the recipient;
  • "Copy text" places the text on the system clipboard.

Apart from these paths, each triggered by your own tap, the app never sends any data off the device.

Your controls

  • Favourites and read marks can each be removed inside the app.
  • Deleting the app erases every record for good — there is no copy anywhere else. For the same reason, deleted data cannot be recovered.

Fonts and texts

  • The two bundled fonts are used under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The full licence text and copyright lines are included in the app under About → Open Source Licences.
  • The source texts are in the public domain (Wang Yangming died in 1529). Their provenance and our editorial policy are stated in the app under About.

Children

This app is intended for a general readership. It is not directed to children under 13 and does not collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds any feature that needs the network or collects data, this policy will be updated first and the change stated clearly in the app. Nothing will be collected before you have seen and agreed to it.
